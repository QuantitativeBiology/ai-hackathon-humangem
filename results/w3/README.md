## 1. Label-free M3 items

**Capacity matrix.** For each task we test, for every distinct KO set touching the support of a feasible WT solution:
- feasibility of the task as written: own inputs, all other boundaries closed (= the P57 rule);
- capacity λ_KO/λ_WT, where λ = the maximal uniform scaling of the task's required outputs/equations (LB > 0), capped at 1000.
  This is *ours*; DESIGN gives no formula.

The exact flux-support shortcut is that of `taskEssentialGenes`. Under Human-GEM task semantics the task part is
medium-independent: the medium enters only through the biomass column, the demands and NGAM. So "per (parent, medium)" =
the same task columns plus a medium-specific biomass sweep (see A-4).

## 2. Sterol branch (label-free; `sterol/`)

All 106 gate-open W2 bounds sets (M0, M1 and M2(f) for 22 documented media, HPLM, Plasmax), plus M0 + cholesterol, × {T0,
B-loo:vitaminD, B-loo:retinoid, both}; every KO solved explicitly.

- **With the biomass as released**, serum cholesterol rescues only DHCR7. FDFT1, SQLE, LSS, NSDHL, EBP, DHCR24 (+ HSD17B7 in H2)
  stay lethal in every medium (the M3_W2 finding, reproduced).
- **With B-loo:vitaminD_catabolites**, every one of them is rescued by serum cholesterol, dose-dependently: KO ratio ≈ f, because
  serum cholesterol is bounded at f × req.
  - lethal at f = 0.001 and 0.01 (H2: 18 of 21 media at f = 0.01);
  - ratio 0.09–0.10 at f = 0.1;
  - full rescue at f = 1 and with M0 + cholesterol at 1000;
  - still lethal in every serum-free medium.

  Retinoid B-loo alone does nothing for the sterol genes.
- **Mevalonate core**: HMGCR, MVK, PMVK, MVD are lethal in all 106 media under all 4 conditions in both parents. **The DESIGN
  control holds** for these sterol conditions. HMGCS1, IDI1, FDPS and GGPS1 are ratio 1 everywhere (GPR/isozyme, as
  pre-registered). The core also stays lethal under serum (RPMI M2 f=1) + every B-add, and on **M0** under the cofactor- and
  metabolite-pool B-loo.
  - **Correction (review M4_W3 §6; flag W3-MEV): it does NOT hold under serum for the cofactor-pool B-loo.** On RPMI M2 f=1
    that variant makes HMGCR/MVK/PMVK/MVD non-lethal (ratio 0.985) in both parents, because ubiquinol, the only non-sterol
    isoprenoid demand, leaves with the pool. The metabolite-pool B-loo keeps the core lethal under serum.
  - Source: `train_summary.json` positive_controls `mevalonate_core_*`.
- **Pyrimidine panel** (`sterol_*_pyrimidine.*`; M3_W5 finding, reported next to the vitamin-D group):
  - with the pool as released, uridine (Plasmax, M0 + uridine) rescues DHODH and UMPS but not CAD in both parents;
  - with B-loo:dihydroorotate, uridine rescues CAD too (ratio 1).

  Without uridine CAD stays lethal in H2. In H1 some thymidine media rescue it via the reversible TYMS (known H1 issue).
- On training labels B-loo:vitaminD is a small gain on M0 (+0.0013) and larger on RPMI M2(f=1), where the sterol genes become
  non-lethal (+0.0086 H1, +0.0090 H2; secondary, medium proxy). B-loo:dihydroorotate is −0.0004 (H1) / 0.0000 (H2) on M0.

control per M0 mode (literal and symmetric). Bootstrap B = 2,000, SEEDS[0]. Full table: `train/mode_scores.tsv`; per line:

  - T1 −0.008 / T2 = T3 −0.015 / T5 −0.029 on H1;

  Only modes adding few, mostly correct calls are ≥ 0: T4_2 = L-T3 (nucleotide synthesis) +0.0013, T4_10 = L-T6 +0.0006,
  L-T4_3 / T4_4 +0.0002. The best task modes beat their pseudo twins (T3 vs pseudo +0.012, CI > 0), but every task mode loses to
  the matched-call-rate control by 0.02–0.32. The task-failure calls are less precise than the gene prior's top calls.
- **Objective modes:** O-NGAM_k ≈ T0 (65 partial ratios move by ≤ 0.03, no call changes; Δ 0 in H1, +0.00002 in H2); O-cap +0.0013 (CI crosses 0); O-MOMA
  −0.002; D-BS −0.001; D-ER 0.
- **B-add:** dolichol-P +0.002; Fe-S cluster (H2) +0.018 (TG pass, loses to the control); GPI precursor −0.011; spermidine
  −0.0007; NAD⁺, GSH and SAM: 0 (no gene changes).
- **Per-task table** (`per_task.tsv`, 57 tasks × parent: failing genes, precision of new calls, Δ of "T0 + task"):

- **Context claims** (`context_claims.tsv`; base medium, serum-free, background groups; one-sided sign-flip, BH over 7,209 tests):

- **Positive controls (label-free):**
  - ETF capability-only FP under T3: **passes in Human2** (ETFA/ETFB/ETFDH called by T3, not by T0) and **fails in Human1**,

- **Binary (fixed cutoff / task call, L-ge06, lineage-equal):** MCC H1 T0 0.334, T3 0.329, B-loo:cofactor 0.379,

## Deviations and ambiguities for the PI

- **D-1 (control definition; post hoc).** The first scoring run used my symmetric matched-call-rate control. Under it no mode
  beats the control (shortlist empty). After seeing those training aggregates I made the **literal DESIGN control** (extra
  calls only) primary and kept the symmetric one as a reported sensitivity. The literal text supports this, but the switch
  happened after viewing training results. Under the symmetric control the W3 shortlist is empty. The superseded run is in
  MANIFEST.tsv.
  - **PI ruling, A11a:** the literal reading is the pre-registered one. For B-loo modes no pre-registered negative control
    applies, so they qualify on TG alone (flag W3-D1).
  - The independent review computed a label-free random-removal control post hoc (review §2): the cofactor-pool variant beats
    it by +0.062 (H1). B2-informed removal is a near-oracle comparator, not a null.
  - The A11 rulings are encoded in `scripts/w3_score_train.py` (`A11_WITHDRAWN`, `A11_FLAGS`). Re-running scoring with them
    reproduced `mode_scores.tsv`, `per_line.tsv`, `context_claims.tsv` and `per_task.tsv` bitwise; only the shortlist,
    summary and binary files changed.
- **D-2 (pseudo controls H1 only).** The Human2 pseudo run stalled on one GLPK LP after ~5.7 CPU-h, before the LP time limit
  existed. A full rerun (~6 CPU-h) did not fit the 25 CPU-h budget, so pseudo-task controls exist for G* = Human1 only; Human2's
  `beats_pseudo` is NA. A 300 s LP time limit with one presolve retry (matching A10) was then added to `w3.solve_status`, and
  the task runner checkpoints per job (`tmp/task_ckpt/`).
- **D-3 (two task vocabularies).** DESIGN W3 defines T0–T5; the final a-priori list §5 (A2) defines different T0–T10 (blind sets,
  "retained"). DESIGN's T0–T5 are implemented as named. The list's sets run as `L-T3`, `L-T4_1..12`, `L-T5`, `L-T5+`, `L-T6`,
  `L-T6+`, `L-T8`, `L-T10`.
  - Its T1 = DESIGN T3; its T2 = DESIGN T1.
  - Its T7 (CellFie scored, not enforced) is a feature, not a mode.
  - Its T9 (should-fail tasks) is a model-quality gate, never an objective; it was not run.
- **D-4 (11 groups).** The Essential file has 11 groups, one of which is "Growth" (GR only). T4_k therefore has 10 informative
  groups; T4_11 ≡ T0 and is not scored separately.
- **A-1** Retinoid and vitamin-D catabolites are two B-loo groups (DESIGN lists them together; the sterol task needs the
  vitamin-D group alone).
- **A-2** O-NGAM uses DESIGN's "k × maximum MAR03964 flux" (bound-limited at 1000), not the final list's "k × WT ATP turnover".
- **A-3** D-ER demands are cytosolic ATP hydrolysis, NADH and NADPH oxidation (*ours*).
- **A-4** The "best documented medium variant" does not exist yet (W2 has not run its TG). The label-free proxy is classic RPMI-1640
  M2(f = 1), gate open: the modal attribution medium. Its signature equals W2's sweep and T0 equals it bitwise. RPMI rows are
  secondary and excluded from the shortlist.
- **A-5** T5 pool = the CellFie consensus tasks. Task activity = mean CellFie score of the task's essential reactions that carry a
  GPR (AND = min, OR = max); active if ≥ 5·ln 2. Tasks without GPR-scored essential reactions are not enforced. Thresholds come
  from fitting-line expression per LOLO fold, over the 3,619 genes of the W1 expression matrix. A line has 31–131 active tasks.
- **A-6** Shortlist size: DESIGN W3 says ≤ 2 (the general §9 rule ≤ 3); 2 are proposed.
- **A-7** Failed MOMA QPs (3 genes) score e = 0 (bottom tied block). DESIGN does not say how a failed single-gene solve is scored.
- **A-8** No run used a pinned worktree (§11.4 asks for one for jobs > 1 CPU-h): the code is uncommitted and I may not commit.
- **A-9** The sweeps' "B-add @1e-3" rows are a sensitivity only (identical to @1e-4 in every Δ to 4 decimals).
